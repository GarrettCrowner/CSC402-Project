Professional Development

West Chester University provides professional development opportunities for individuals and teams to boost employee performance, improve confidence and knowledge in skills, increase productivity, and enhance team dynamics. WCU is invested in your success and dedicated to cultivating employee and team advancement.

Contact Training and Organizational Development

Services Offered

Team Strengthening Workshops

Effective team strengthening builds trust, encourages communication, and increases collaboration and productivity. Organizational Development provides meaningful opportunities for your team to connect, learn, and grow together.

VIEW OUR MOST POPULAR WORKSHOPS

Departmental Retreats

A retreat from the day to day is an effective way to focus on an area or issue, problem solve, improve processes and plans, or bond with colleagues through team strengthening activities. Organizational Development supports your retreat by planning and facilitating content and helping to plan the event logistics. 

CONTACT ORGANIZATIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Personalized Learning Paths

LinkedIn Learning develops and enhances skills through short, expert-led online courses. Organizational Development can create personalized LinkedIn Learning Paths related to your field and interests using focused, self-paced, video instruction and utilizing over 16,000 online courses. Contact orgdev@wcupa.edu to design a learning path for you or members of your department.

Employee Onboarding

West Chester University offers several integrated onboarding programs and initiatives that provide newly hired employees with the information and resources they need to adjust to the social and performance aspects of their new role. Employees are encouraged to participate in all programs.

EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING

Tuition Waiver and Reimbursement

West Chester University promotes lifelong learning by offering tuition waiver and reimbursement for eligible employees and their eligible dependents. Our degree and certificate programs provide opportunities for employees and their dependents to enhance their knowledge and skills for advancement. The tuition benefit covers tuition and select applicable fees.

Learn more about Tuition and Waiver Reimbursement

Training and Program Calendar

Organizational Development offers 30-minute live zoom training on important topics related to WCU policy, programs, professional development, and benefits. Sessions include an overview and allow time for Q&A.

VIEW THE CALENDAR AND REGISTER

LinkedIn Learning

LinkedIn Learning offers over 16,000 on-demand expert-led, short, focused, and self-paced, video instruction online courses; content for professional certification exams preparation; and continuing education credit courses that are free to WCU employees. Browse the full list of online business, creative, and technology courses.

Faculty and Staff Training (FAST)

FAST training supports WCU staff and faculty to gain proficiency and learn best practices in trending platforms including Office 365, OneDrive, D2L, and RamCloud. Courses are designed to accommodate all levels of learning and allow users to build on acquired skills.

LEARN MORE ABOUT FAST

Equal Opportunity and Compliance

The Office of Equal Opportunity and Compliance (OEOC) offers educational and learning opportunities for all faculty, staff, and students at West Chester University.  In accordance with the University’s strategic plan, it is WCU’s intent to foster a campus environment that invites all community members to build upon their awareness, skills, and competencies related to diversity and inclusion topic areas.

ACCESS EOC TRAINING

Twardowski Career Development Center

In partnership with the Office of Training and Organizational Development, The Twardowski Career Development Center supports employees by offering services that include professional headshots; developing your resume and interviewing skills; and building a career strategy to obtain your professional development goals.

LEARN MORE ABOUT THE Twardowski Career Development Center
